✅ SOLUTION 2: UNBLOCK THE EXECUTABLE

If you want to use nepali.exe directly:

Method A: Properties Unblock
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Right-click on: dist\nepali.exe
2. Select "Properties"
3. Check "Unblock" at the bottom
4. Click "Apply"
5. Click "OK"

Method B: PowerShell Unblock
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:

powershell -Command "Unblock-File -Path 'C:\Users\Diwas\Desktop\.np lannguage\dist\nepali.exe'"

Method C: Windows Defender Exclusion
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Open Windows Security
2. Go to "Virus & threat protection"
3. Click "Manage settings"
4. Scroll to "Exclusions"
5. Click "Add or remove exclusions"
6. Add: C:\Users\Diwas\Desktop\.np lannguage\dist\nepali.exe
